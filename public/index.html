Frequently asked questions

Is this a real investment platform?

This downloadable MVP is a working software prototype. Its payment flow is deliberately simulated and does not move real money.

Can M-Pesa be connected?

Yes. The backend is structured so a server-side Daraja payment adapter can be added after you have the approved business configuration and production readiness.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. The product UI intentionally avoids promising guaranteed returns. Actual investment terms and risk disclosures must come from the approved product.
